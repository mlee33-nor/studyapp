Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

StudyBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with a random salt and stored only on your device.

Usage Data: We collect study session data (duration, categories, streaks, and completion status) to provide you with statistics and progress tracking. This data is stored locally on your device.

No Analytics or Tracking: StudyBuddy does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or browsing history.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All user data is stored locally on your device using browser storage. We do not transmit your personal data to any external servers. Your study history, preferences, and account credentials remain on your device at all times.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Browsing history
  • Health or fitness data

4. Third-Party Services

StudyBuddy loads visual assets (animations and fonts) from the following trusted CDN services:

  • LottieFiles (lottiefiles.com) — for animation assets
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — for typography

These services may collect standard web request data (IP address, user agent) as part of serving content. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

5. In-App Purchases

Premium features are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information. All transactions are handled securely by Apple in accordance with their privacy practices.

6. Children's Privacy

StudyBuddy is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only personal data collected (email address) is provided voluntarily during account creation.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the Settings screen by using the "Reset All Stats" feature, or by logging out (which clears all stored data). You may also uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your information. You can:

  • View all your stored data through the app's interface
  • Delete all data through Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data completely

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
